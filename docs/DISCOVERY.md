# Saved offers, watchlists and readiness

Discover retains the provider library and owner-added sites. Prepare a stay or
route search opens supported search parameters at Airbnb, Booking.com or
Rome2Rio. Use preceding planned destination chooses the latest relevant confirmed
stay/transport arrival. Review the suggested origin: it is not physical tracking.
Other directory links remain source links without invented query capabilities.

Save an offer as accommodation or transportation. Record the source, exact travel
dates, currency, payable price, mandatory fee evidence, facilities, availability,
cancellation conditions and checked date. A saved offer is separate from a
confirmed booking. Blank facilities, fees and route costs remain unknown.

Select two or more offers and open Compare. Temporary dates and monthly target,
stretch cap and facility requirements apply to that comparison only. Stay prices
are compared only for identical dates/currencies; Atlas does not invent a monthly
discount or extrapolate a date-specific quote. Explanations show requirements,
workspace evidence, affordability, freshness, known cost and recorded interests.
A recorded stretch justification never automatically increases the cap.

Transport comparisons show known fare/baggage/transfer/overnight cost components
and use the planning service for duration, check-in and work compatibility.
Unknown additions prevent a complete-cost claim; different currencies and routes
still require owner review. There is no live inventory or global-cheapest claim.

Preview budget with this offer uses exact period/month accounting on an in-memory
scenario. You can choose an existing booking to replace hypothetically. The
preview shows period/daily forecasts and remaining flexible funds; bookings and
payments are not saved. Missing costs remain outside the known estimate.

Save watchlist entries for stays, transport, destinations, experiences or practical
resources. Source snapshot & recheck records a manual price/availability snapshot,
notes and time without silently changing a previously quoted price. Open the
source to verify it and add a timezone-aware recheck reminder. No automated price
monitor or historical price chart is claimed.

Trip Readiness opens from Calendar. It groups saved confirmation, payment,
cancellation, work, gap, document, workspace, review-draft, refund and reminder
items into attention/upcoming/complete/unknown. Select an item to open its local
record or workspace. Suggested booking reminders are explicit; optional rules
can be enabled in Settings → Notifications. Delivery requires a running host.
